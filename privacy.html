Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

The short version: TrailSync stores the account and ride details you give it so it can sync them across your devices. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools. Card payments are handled by Stripe, and we never see your card number.

This policy explains what TrailSync ("we", "us") collects when you use the TrailSync web app at trailsync123.netlify.app, how it is used, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our login provider, Supabase, and we can't read them.
  • Ride logs you enter: trail systems and trail names, trail conditions and surfaces, tire pressures, suspension clicker settings, setup ratings, notes, and dates.
  • Bike profiles you enter: bike names, fork and shock models, riding style, tire setup, and your rider weight.
  • Payment status: when you buy TrailSync Pro, Stripe processes the payment. We receive your Stripe customer ID, the plan you chose, its status (for example active, canceled or lifetime) and its renewal date. We do not receive or store card numbers.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting and service providers automatically receive your IP address, browser type and the time of each request, and may keep these in their logs for security and reliability.

Data stored on your device

TrailSync saves your rides, bike profile and login session in your browser's local storage, so the app can open and log rides without signal. Changes made offline are queued on your device and uploaded when you're back online. We don't use cookies for advertising or analytics.

How we use your information

  • To run your account and sync your rides and bikes across your devices.
  • To show your stats, trends and setup recommendations. Recommendations are calculated in your browser from your own data.
  • To process payments and unlock TrailSync Pro.
  • To send account emails, such as sign-up confirmation and password resets.
  • To answer your questions and keep the service secure.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run TrailSync. Each one only gets what it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase: login, account emails and database storage.
  • Stripe: payment processing. Stripe's use of your payment details is covered by the Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • Netlify: website hosting and the server functions that handle checkout.
  • Content delivery networks (jsDelivr, Tailwind CSS and Google Fonts): these load parts of the app's code and fonts, and receive your IP address when they do.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of TrailSync and its users. If TrailSync is ever sold or transferred, your information may move with it under this policy.

Our providers may store and process data in the United States and other countries.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and ride data until you delete it or ask us to delete your account. Stripe keeps payment records for as long as the law requires, even after your account is deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • You can edit your bike profile and delete individual rides in the app at any time.
  • Pro members can export all their rides to CSV or PDF.
  • To get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account and everything in it, email us at trailsync.help@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR may give you further rights. We honor these requests for every user, wherever you live.

Security

Data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Database rules mean each signed-in rider can only read and change their own rides and profile. Secret keys are kept on the server, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Children

TrailSync is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app or by email.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email trailsync.help@gmail.com.
